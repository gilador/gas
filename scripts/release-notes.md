**Install**
1. Download the `.pkg` below and open it.
2. gas is free and open source, so it isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm: click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway**.
3. When you update, macOS asks to let Installer modify apps. Click **Allow**.

Already on gas 1.0.24 or later? It updates itself from inside the app.
